# eBay AI Listing Studio Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 29 September 2026**

eBay AI Listing Studio is desktop software designed to assist users with creating, managing and publishing listings to eBay.

## Information We Process

The application may process information provided by the user, including listing information, product photographs, listing preferences and application settings.

The application may also access information provided by eBay where necessary to create, manage and publish listings.

## eBay Account Access

Access to an eBay account is provided using eBay's OAuth authorization system.

eBay AI Listing Studio does not require or store the user's eBay password.

## Data Storage

Application data may be stored locally on the user's computer. This may include listing information, settings and authorization information required for the application to operate.

## Third-Party Services

The application may communicate with services including eBay and OpenAI where required to provide listing creation and management functionality.

## Account Deletion

The application supports eBay Marketplace Account Deletion notifications and will process applicable requests in accordance with eBay requirements.

## Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials and application data.

## Changes to this Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as the application develops.
